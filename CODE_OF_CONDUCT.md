# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OpenKartLine welcomes people of every background and experience level. We expect participation to be respectful, constructive, patient, and focused on improving the project.

Positive behavior includes:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback.
- Explaining technical assumptions and uncertainty.
- Welcoming questions and different levels of experience.
- Crediting sources, prior work, data owners, and contributors.
- Respecting privacy, safety, licensing, and circuit rules.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, publishing private information, or repeatedly disrupting collaboration.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository activity, community spaces, project events, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact information on their GitHub profiles. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation depending on impact and pattern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.

Maintainers are also subject to this policy. A maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves where another maintainer is available.
