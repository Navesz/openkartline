# Funding and donations

OpenKartLine does not currently advertise a donation channel. The repository owner's GitHub Sponsors page is not active, and the project will not invent a payment handle or send contributors to an unverified account.

Contributing code, tests, documentation, translation, permitted datasets, design work, and careful review is the best way to support the project today.

## Activation checklist

Before adding `.github/FUNDING.yml` or a donation button, maintainers must:

1. choose an account or fiscal host that can legally receive funds in its jurisdiction;
2. verify ownership and payout access directly;
3. publish who controls the funds and how decisions are made;
4. define acceptable uses, fees, tax responsibility, and refund limitations;
5. separate donations from promises of safety, accuracy, support, or roadmap priority;
6. disclose recurring project expenses and material conflicts of interest;
7. test the public link without being signed in.

Once a verified GitHub Sponsors profile exists, add only the confirmed username:

```yaml
github: [Navesz]
```

That example is intentionally not active configuration. Other providers must not be added without a verified project-owned URL.

## Transparency policy

Funding does not buy technical outcomes, favorable research results, merged code, private access to telemetry, or priority over safety and licensing. Material sponsorship related to a proposed dependency, kart vendor, circuit, dataset, or validation claim must be disclosed in the relevant issue or pull request.

If donations become material, maintainers will publish periodic summaries of gross receipts, platform fees, major expenses, and the remaining project balance while protecting donor privacy. Typical permitted uses may include domains, signing certificates, CI capacity, test hardware, licensed development tools, accessibility audits, and acquisition of redistributable validation data.
